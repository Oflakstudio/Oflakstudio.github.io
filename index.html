About

Design that removes noise
so the message lands first.

Manish Kumar is a graphic and visual communication designer, and the founder of Oflak Studio & Crea Graphix. Over 3+ years he has delivered 25+ branding projects across 11 industries — from healthcare and B2B technology to retail interiors, automotive, food & FMCG, wellness and NGO communication.


His work spans brand identity, logo and typography systems, marketing collateral, social-media creatives and digital campaigns. Alongside his studio, he has designed corporate B2B communication at Suprams Info Solutions and led healthcare campaigns as Digital Marketing Manager at JGM Multispecialty Hospital. He pairs creative craft with business communication, guided by a simple idea: Creativity. Simplified.
